Your login stays with Instagram.

INSTA_LITE has no server, analytics or tracking. It does not collect your password or store your messages. Preferences, timer and 30 days of counters are saved in this browser. Instagram still handles and stores your account and conversations.

Permissions: Instagram pages for redirects and interface controls; local storage for preferences and counts; alarms for the timer. It never uses Instagram’s private API.

Works in desktop Chrome and Edge. It does not change the Instagram mobile app. If a composer or distracting panel appears after an Instagram update, stop using the restrictive mode and report the page layout (without private messages).

Independent project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram or Meta.
