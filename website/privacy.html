Your inbox is yours.

INSTA_LITE is an independent browser extension that modifies the Instagram website on your computer. It has no account service or extension backend.

What stays in your browser

The extension stores your enabled setting, messaging mode, shared-link preference, appearance preference, focus timer and daily counters in chrome.storage.local. Counters contain dates, the number of redirected visits and active inbox seconds. They are retained for 30 local calendar days.

Clear counters from All settings. Removing the extension removes its local data. Your data is not synced by this extension.

What the extension does on Instagram

It checks navigation URLs and page elements to redirect distractions, hide navigation and optionally hide composers. It does not collect passwords, copy message bodies, store conversations or call Instagram’s private API. The code does not send analytics or data to an external service.

Your Instagram account

You sign in directly on instagram.com. Instagram handles your credentials, cookies, conversations and reactions under its own policies. INSTA_LITE does not remove Instagram’s tracking or change its data practices.

Permissions

  • Instagram host access: redirects and local page layout changes.
  • Declarative network request: redirect distracting page visits.
  • Local storage: preferences, timer and counters.
  • Alarms: timer completion and badge updates.

This website

The companion website includes no analytics, external fonts or tracking scripts. Its inbox preview uses fictional messages and does not connect to Instagram. Your hosting provider may process ordinary request logs.

Compatibility and control

This is a focus aid, not a security control. Instagram may change its layout or features. Turn off the distraction blocker or remove the extension at any time. Report compatibility issues in the GitHub repository without including credentials or private conversation contents.
